Ledgermail

Card charges into Notion, from the emails you already get.

Your card company emails you every charge. Ledgermail reads those emails and files each one into a Notion database you own. It never logs in to your bank.

Read the code

What happens

Your card sends a charge emailas it does today
Ledgermail files it in Notiondate, store, amount, and a guessed category
The line appears in Discordstore, amount, category
Fix the category from the messageif the guess is wrong

Scope

  • No bank login, no aggregation service. It reads notification emails only.
  • The ledger is a Notion database you control. Export or delete it anytime.
  • Supports the card issuers it has parsers for: Sumitomo Mitsui, Rakuten Card, View Card. Parsers live in the repository and are the part you would change.
  • Limitations (foreign currency, refunds, same-day same-amount charges) are listed in the setup guide. The guide is in Japanese.
  • Verified end to end with Sumitomo Mitsui only. Rakuten and View Card matching, and Discord posting with a real bot token, are not yet confirmed on the shipped code.
  • The demo video was made for a product pitch. It shows a weekly report, a "learned from your tap" badge, a Split button, and US dollar amounts. None of these are in the published code, which reads Japanese-yen notifications from the three issuers above. The video also ends with a "Join the waitlist" button. There is no waitlist; the product is on hold.

The code is public

The part that does the work is published as ledger-starter and runs on Google Apps Script and Notion. The Ledgermail product itself is on hold.

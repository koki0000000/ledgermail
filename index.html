What Ledgermail does — and doesn't — touch

The honest scope, up front.

No bank connection. It reads the notification emails your card company already sends.
  • We never ask for your online banking username or password
  • We never connect to Plaid or any bank-aggregation service
  • Your ledger lives in a Notion database you control — export or delete anytime
  • Today it supports a limited set of card issuers — the ones whose notification emails we've built parsers for. Don't see yours? The parsers are in the repository below — they're the part you'd change.

vs. YNAB / Copilot Money / Lunch Money / Monarch: those ask you to link your bank or enter transactions by hand. Ledgermail asks for neither — it reads the emails you're already getting.

The working part is open source

Ledgermail was an attempt to turn this into a product. That attempt is on hold — but the piece that does the actual work is published, and it runs today on Google Apps Script and Notion. No waitlist, no signup, no bank connection.
